## 🗂️ Other projects

Most of my work is built for clients, so the code stays in private repos. Here's a small taste of what's in there:

#### 📦 Multi-store stock hub
Five WooCommerce stores share one stock: a hub keeps an auditable ledger and the others claim stock per order line. Stock entries with a barcode scanner, and stock checks from ChatGPT or Claude through MCP.

#### 🏠 Real estate listings sync
Properties and developments from Casafari and InmoVilla into WordPress on a schedule, with images, multilingual taxonomies and faceted search filters per language.

#### 🧾 EU VAT exemption engine
B2B exemption only after a live VIES check (cached), special territories like the Canary Islands handled on their own, and fixed gross prices whatever VAT applies.

#### 🚗 Vehicle tax subscription platform
A customer portal where people pay monthly and their yearly vehicle tax is handled for them: Stripe, Multibanco and MB WAY, a public tax simulator and a backoffice.

#### 🔎 Warehouse picking
Every unit scanned by EAN before an order ships, wrong or missing items flagged on the spot, and serial numbers recorded per line and printed on the invoice.

#### 💇 B2B area for a WooCommerce store
A closed area for professionals with its own tiered pricing, exclusive catalogue and shipping rules, alongside the public store.

#### 🧩 Store toolkit
Snippets copied by hand between five stores, rewritten as one plugin with switchable modules: auto-cancel of unpaid orders, stock-aware payment methods, WPML price and stock sync.
